HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Rotate and move falling blocks to fill complete horizontal lines. Full lines disappear and award points.


Special Features:

  • Ghost Piece: Shows where the piece will land.
  • Super Rotation System (SRS): Wall kicks allow rotating against walls and obstacles.
  • Hold Queue: Press C or Shift to swap and save a piece for later.
  • Hard Drop: Press Space to instantly drop and lock.
  • Tetris Bonus: Clearing 4 lines at once awards massive bonus points!
